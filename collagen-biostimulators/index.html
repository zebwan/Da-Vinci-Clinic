Collagen biostimulator treatment in Kuala Lumpur

Biostimulators restore volume now and keep improving for months as your own collagen rebuilds. Da Vinci offers eight formulations for the face, neck, hands and body, performed by LCP-certified doctors who train other doctors on Ellansé.

What we use

Biostimulators we offer

Different carriers and particles suit different areas and timelines.

Ellansé (PCL)

Polycaprolactone microspheres for cheeks, temples, jawline, hands and lines, with immediate support and gradual collagen growth.

Radiesse and AestheFill

Calcium hydroxylapatite (Radiesse) and PDLLA (AestheFill) for volume loss, neck wrinkles and facial contour.

Lanluma, Lenisna, Collaju, Deusaderm

PLLA for face and body (Lanluma V and X), PDLLA plus crosslinked HA (Lenisna), injectable collagen (Collaju) and Deusaderm LIDO.
