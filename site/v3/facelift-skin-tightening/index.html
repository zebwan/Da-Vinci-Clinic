Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers from the Da Vinci doctors.

Both use ultrasound energy. Ultherapy Prime is FDA-cleared for lifting the face and neck and adds real-time imaging so the doctor sees the tissue layer before delivering energy. HIFU devices vary in quality and depth control, so not all HIFU is equivalent to Ultherapy Prime.

Ultherapy Prime results typically last 12 to 18 months depending on skin condition, age and lifestyle. Oligio X and Thermage results are quoted at about one year, HIFU at about six months.

Energy-based lifting has none to minimal downtime: mild redness or tingling that settles within hours. Thread lifts involve three to seven days of swelling.

DV™ SynergyLift is planned as one to two sessions, varying by treatment. Maintenance is usually every 12 to 18 months.

Numbing cream is applied for ultrasound treatments. XERF and Density use built-in cooling, so numbing cream is not usually required.

All four clinics: Mid Valley, Bukit Jalil, Cheras and TRX. Ultherapy Prime availability is confirmed when you book.
