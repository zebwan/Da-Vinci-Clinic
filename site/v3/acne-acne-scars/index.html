What we use

Treatments for acne and scars

Chosen by whether your concern is active acne, redness, or depressed scars.

AdvaTX yellow laser

A 589 nm solid-state yellow laser that reduces acne-causing bacteria and redness with no numbing and no downtime. First in Malaysia at Da Vinci.

Pico laser and fractional CO2

PicoSure Pro honeycomb and fractional CO2 resurfacing for pitted and depressed scars.

Potenza, EndyMed and BTL Exion

RF microneedling to remodel collagen in textured scars, plus medical extraction, OxyGeneo facial, Silk Peel and chemical peels for active breakouts.
