Treatment

Hair loss treatment in Kuala Lumpur

DV™ Hair is a non-surgical hair restoration protocol: laser and microneedling to stimulate the scalp, regenerative injectables to feed the follicle, light therapy and prescribed medication to hold the result. Laser hair removal is available for unwanted hair.

Treatment details

What we use

Hair treatments we use

Combined in sequence rather than offered one at a time.

Fotona HAIRestart

Er:YAG laser on the Fotona SP Dynamis Pro to stimulate follicles and scalp circulation.

Polynucleotides, exosomes and PRP

Plinest, exosome and platelet-rich plasma scalp injections for follicle regeneration.

PDT light, microneedling and medication

PDT Healite 633 nm therapy, microneedling and prescribed oral or topical medication.

Who it is for

Thinning, receding hairlines and pattern hair loss

• Thinning crown and receding hairline
