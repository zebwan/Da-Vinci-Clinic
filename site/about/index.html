Our story

Da Vinci Clinic began in Mid Valley City in 2006 with a simple idea: aesthetic medicine should be practised as medicine, with a doctor assessing first and selling second.

That approach built a following. The clinic became the first in Malaysia to own the PicoSure Pro laser, recorded by the Malaysia Book of Records, and the first in the country to offer the AdvaTX yellow laser. It is a certified Ultherapy Prime and Thermage provider and Malaysia’s top Ultherapy Prime user.

Branches followed in Bukit Jalil (DV Prestige), Cheras and, in 2026, Tun Razak Exchange.

How we practise

Every patient is seen by a doctor, never by a sales consultant. Options and pricing are laid out clearly and you decide.

Energy devices are FDA-cleared and every injectable comes from its original maker. Our doctors hold the Ministry of Health’s LCP credential and keep training: Merz, Fotona, Neauvia, Ellansé and Plinest all use Da Vinci doctors as trainers or key opinion leaders.
