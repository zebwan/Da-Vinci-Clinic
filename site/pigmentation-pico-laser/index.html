Pigmentation, melasma and pico laser treatment in Kuala Lumpur

DV™ PigmentAway clears melasma, sun spots, freckles and tattoos with picosecond lasers that shatter pigment mechanically rather than with heat. Da Vinci was the first clinic in Malaysia to own PicoSure Pro, recorded by the Malaysia Book of Records.

What we use

Three pico lasers, matched to your pigment

Each laser is matched to your skin type and the depth of the pigment before a single pulse is fired.

PicoSure Pro

The world’s first 755 nm alexandrite picosecond laser, with the honeycomb handpiece for acne scars, wrinkles and melasma.

PicoWay

Four picosecond wavelengths (1064, 785, 730 and 532 nm) for multi-coloured tattoos and stubborn pigmentation.

Fotona StarWalker PQX

Fotona’s highest-energy pico laser with FracTAT technology for tattoos, scars and rejuvenation.
